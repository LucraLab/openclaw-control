20.1.1 Introduction and Penalty Relief This section covers penalty abatement procedures including reasonable cause and first time abate (FTA) provisions for taxpayers who have accrued penalties on their accounts. 20.1.1.3 Reasonable Cause Reasonable cause relief is generally granted when the taxpayer exercised ordinary business care and prudence but was nevertheless unable to comply with a prescribed duty within the prescribed time. Common Reasonable Cause Arguments Death, serious illness, or unavoidable absence of the taxpayer or immediate family Fire, casualty, natural disaster, or other disturbance Inability to obtain records Erroneous advice from the IRS Ignorance of the law (in limited circumstances) 20.1.1.4 First Time Abate (FTA) The First Time Abate administrative waiver provides relief from penalties for taxpayers who have a clean compliance history for the 3 tax years prior to the penalty year. The taxpayer must have filed all currently required returns or filed a valid extension. 20.1.1.5 Statutory Exceptions Certain statutory provisions provide for penalty abatement including reliance on written IRS advice under IRC 6404(f).
