Form 656 Offer in Compromise The taxpayer submits an offer in compromise to settle tax debt for less than the full amount owed. Doubt as to collectibility is the most common basis for an offer in compromise. The Reasonable Collection Potential (RCP) determines the minimum acceptable offer amount. RCP equals Future Income plus Net Equity in Assets. Application fee is $205 which is waivable for low-income taxpayers. Initial payment is 20% of lump sum offer or first monthly payment for periodic payment offers.
