5.14.1 Securing Installment Agreements

This section provides guidance on installment agreement procedures for taxpayers who cannot pay their tax liabilities in full. An installment agreement allows the taxpayer to make monthly payments over time.

5.14.1.1 Overview

The IRS may enter into a written agreement with any taxpayer under which the taxpayer is allowed to make payment on any tax in installment payments if the IRS determines that the installment agreement will facilitate full or partial collection of the tax liability.

5.14.1.2 Types of Installment Agreements

  • Guaranteed Installment Agreements - for balances under $10,000
  • Streamlined Installment Agreements - for balances under $50,000
  • Non-Streamlined Installment Agreements - require financial analysis
  • Partial Payment Installment Agreements (PPIA)

5.14.1.3 Financial Analysis Required

For non-streamlined agreements where the balance exceeds $50,000, a complete financial analysis using Form 433-A (Collection Information Statement for Wage Earners and Self-Employed Individuals) is required.
