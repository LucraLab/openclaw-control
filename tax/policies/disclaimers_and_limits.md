# Disclaimers and Limits

Version: 1.0.0
Status: USER-FACING

## Primary Disclaimers

**NOT LEGAL OR TAX ADVICE**

The Tax Pod provides informational decision support only. All outputs must be verified by licensed tax professionals (CPAs, enrolled agents, tax attorneys) before taking action.

**NOT A SUBSTITUTE FOR PROFESSIONAL REPRESENTATION**

- Cannot represent you before the IRS
- Cannot file tax returns or forms on your behalf
- Cannot provide legal opinions or interpretations of tax law
- Cannot guarantee outcomes or results

**NO ATTORNEY-CLIENT OR CPA-CLIENT RELATIONSHIP**

Use of the Tax Pod does not create a professional relationship. You remain responsible for:
- Verifying all information
- Meeting IRS deadlines
- Complying with tax law
- Hiring qualified professionals when needed

## System Limitations

**WHAT THE TAX POD CAN DO:**

- Triage IRS notices and suggest next steps
- Calculate installment agreement eligibility estimates
- Identify cost segregation study opportunities
- Generate structured handoff packages for tax preparers
- Provide educational information on IRS procedures

**WHAT THE TAX POD CANNOT DO:**

- File tax returns or IRS forms
- Represent you in IRS audits or appeals
- Provide legal advice or tax law interpretations
- Guarantee acceptance of payment plans or offers
- Store or process full Social Security Numbers
- Make decisions on your behalf
- Bypass the need for licensed professionals

## Accuracy and Currency

**Information Currency:**
- Tax law changes frequently
- IRS procedures and forms update annually
- Vault knowledge sources have last-updated timestamps
- Always verify current rules with IRS.gov or your tax professional

**Calculation Accuracy:**
- Estimates are approximations, not official determinations
- IRS makes final decisions on eligibility and amounts
- User inputs affect accuracy (garbage in, garbage out)
- Professional calculation may differ

## User Responsibilities

**YOU MUST:**

1. Provide accurate information (estimates acceptable where noted)
2. Verify all outputs with a licensed tax professional
3. Meet all IRS deadlines (system does not track or remind)
4. File all required tax returns before pursuing payment plans
5. Understand that you remain legally responsible for tax obligations
6. Protect your own sensitive information (do not share full SSN, bank accounts)

**YOU SHOULD NOT:**

1. Rely solely on Tax Pod outputs for important tax decisions
2. Assume calculations are IRS-approved or binding
3. Delay seeking professional help for urgent matters
4. Share login credentials or evidence records with unauthorized parties
5. Provide information you are uncomfortable storing (even temporarily)

## Urgency and Deadlines

**CRITICAL URGENCY NOTICES:**
- Levy notices (bank/wage garnishment)
- Lien filing notices
- Final Notice of Intent to Levy

**If you receive these: Contact a tax professional immediately. Do not rely on the Tax Pod as your only resource.**

The system flags urgency levels but does not track deadlines or send reminders. You are responsible for monitoring IRS correspondence and meeting response deadlines.

## Payment Plan First Philosophy

The Tax Pod prioritizes getting you into IRS-compliant payment arrangements quickly to:
- Stop penalties and interest from accumulating
- Prevent collection actions (levies, liens)
- Buy time for professional review and optimization

**This does NOT mean:**
- Payment plans are always the best option
- You should skip professional consultation
- The first plan offered is optimal

Always have a tax professional review before committing to long-term agreements.

## Data Privacy

**What We Collect:**
- Case classification and tax year information
- Financial estimates (income, expenses, amounts owed)
- Last 4 digits of SSN only (never full SSN)
- User summaries (sanitized, no unnecessary PII)

**What We Do NOT Collect:**
- Full Social Security Numbers
- Bank account or credit card numbers
- Tax return attachments containing full SSN
- Unnecessary personal identifiers

Refer to `data_minimization.md` and `redaction_rules.md` for details.

## When to Seek Professional Help Immediately

**Red Flags Requiring CPA/Attorney:**

1. Criminal tax investigation or fraud allegations
2. Large amounts owed (over $100k)
3. Complex business structures or international issues
4. Audit or appeals with significant amounts at stake
5. IRS determination letters or final decisions
6. Offers in Compromise (requires expert preparation)
7. Any situation involving potential penalties or jail time

**The Tax Pod is NOT a substitute for professional representation in these scenarios.**

## Questions or Concerns

If you are uncertain whether the Tax Pod is appropriate for your situation:
1. Consult the `safe_answering_rules.md` policy
2. Contact a licensed tax professional
3. Review IRS.gov official guidance

**When in doubt, seek professional help.**
