# Tax Pod Vetting Checklist

Version: 1.0.0

This checklist helps users and tax professionals verify Tax Pod outputs before taking action.

---

## For Users (Before Acting)

### Input Accuracy

- [ ] All amounts (owed, income, expenses) are accurate to best of my knowledge
- [ ] Tax years listed are complete (no missing years)
- [ ] Case classification matches my situation
- [ ] I have disclosed all relevant IRS correspondence
- [ ] I have not withheld information that might affect recommendations

### Assumptions Verification

- [ ] I have reviewed all stated assumptions in the output
- [ ] Assumptions marked "NOT VERIFIED" have been checked independently
- [ ] I understand what was NOT evaluated in the analysis
- [ ] I am aware of edge cases not addressed

### Professional Consultation

- [ ] I have contacted a licensed tax professional (CPA/EA/attorney)
- [ ] Professional has reviewed Tax Pod recommendations
- [ ] Professional agrees (or has provided alternative recommendation)
- [ ] I understand the risks disclosed in the output
- [ ] I am not relying solely on Tax Pod output for important decisions

### Urgency and Deadlines

- [ ] I am aware of any response deadlines
- [ ] I have enough time to seek professional help before deadline
- [ ] I understand consequences of missing deadlines
- [ ] CRITICAL URGENCY: I have contacted professional within 24-48 hours

---

## For Tax Professionals (When Reviewing)

### Source Verification

- [ ] IRS publications cited are current (check revision year)
- [ ] Tax code sections referenced are accurate
- [ ] Case law or guidance cited (if any) is still good law
- [ ] No outdated information or superseded rules

### Calculation Accuracy

- [ ] Payment plan calculations are mathematically correct
- [ ] IRS allowable expense standards match current year
- [ ] Collection statute expiration date (CSED) considered
- [ ] Interest and penalty calculations verified (or flagged as estimates)

### Option Completeness

- [ ] Relevant options have been presented (IA, OIC, CNC, etc.)
- [ ] No better alternatives overlooked
- [ ] Risks and limitations disclosed appropriately
- [ ] User can realistically execute recommended option

### Compliance Check

- [ ] Recommendation does not violate tax law
- [ ] No tax evasion or avoidance schemes suggested
- [ ] Proper professional engagement recommended where needed
- [ ] User understands they need professional representation

### Missing Analysis

- [ ] Passive activity loss limitations considered (if applicable)
- [ ] State tax implications noted (if relevant)
- [ ] Penalty abatement opportunities evaluated
- [ ] Innocent spouse relief considered (if married filing jointly)
- [ ] Audit reconsideration if liability disputed
- [ ] Statute of limitations on collection checked

---

## Red Flags (Stop and Consult Pro Immediately)

### User Should STOP if:

- [ ] Recommendation involves amounts > $100k
- [ ] IRS notice mentions criminal investigation or fraud
- [ ] Deadline is < 15 days and no professional engaged
- [ ] Calculated payment is unaffordable
- [ ] Tax Pod output contradicts IRS correspondence
- [ ] Any uncertainty about legality or compliance

### Professional Should Escalate if:

- [ ] Tax Pod output contains factual errors
- [ ] Sources cited are outdated or incorrect
- [ ] Calculation methodology is flawed
- [ ] Risks not adequately disclosed
- [ ] User misunderstood critical assumptions
- [ ] Better options exist that were not presented

---

## Evidence Record Review

### For Users:

- [ ] I have a copy of the evidence record (case_id, timestamp, sources)
- [ ] My PII has been properly redacted in logs/proofs
- [ ] I understand what information was used in the analysis
- [ ] I can provide this evidence to my tax professional

### For Professionals:

- [ ] Evidence record contains sufficient detail for independent analysis
- [ ] All assumptions are documented
- [ ] Sources are citeable and verifiable
- [ ] No prohibited data (full SSN, bank accounts) present
- [ ] Audit trail is complete and immutable

---

## Final Verification

### Before User Takes Action:

**ASK YOURSELF:**

1. Have I verified all inputs are accurate?
2. Have I reviewed this with a licensed tax professional?
3. Do I understand the risks and limitations?
4. Can I afford the recommended payment (if applicable)?
5. Am I acting within IRS deadlines?
6. Do I have a backup plan if this fails?

**IF ANY ANSWER IS "NO" → STOP AND CONSULT PROFESSIONAL FIRST.**

---

### Before Professional Endorses:

**ASK YOURSELF:**

1. Is the Tax Pod output accurate and current?
2. Are there better options for this client?
3. Is the recommended action in client's best interest?
4. Does client understand their responsibilities?
5. Are risks adequately disclosed?
6. Is my professional liability exposure acceptable?

**IF ANY ANSWER IS "NO" → PROVIDE CORRECTIVE GUIDANCE.**

---

## Documentation

### User Should Retain:

- [ ] Tax Pod evidence record (case_id)
- [ ] Tax Pod response output (full text)
- [ ] All IRS correspondence
- [ ] Professional consultation notes
- [ ] Decision log (why I chose this option)

### Professional Should Document:

- [ ] Tax Pod output review (date, findings)
- [ ] Independent verification of calculations
- [ ] Client consultation and advice given
- [ ] Engagement letter (if representing client)
- [ ] Follow-up plan and deadlines

---

## Emergency Situations

**IF RECEIVING:**

- Letter 1058 (Final Notice of Intent to Levy)
- CP90/CP297 (Intent to Seize Assets)
- Any criminal investigation notice
- Levy or lien already filed

**THEN:**

1. Contact licensed tax professional within 24 hours
2. Do NOT rely on Tax Pod as only resource
3. Preserve all IRS correspondence
4. Do NOT ignore deadlines (Collection Due Process hearing rights)
5. Consider representation by tax attorney (not just CPA/EA)

---

**END OF CHECKLIST**

Users and professionals should complete relevant sections before acting on Tax Pod recommendations.
